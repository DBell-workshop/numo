## About ClarSum

ClarSum is built around three ways to do math, so the right one fits
whatever your hands are doing:

1. **Tap it** — keypad with ≥ 44pt touch targets and WCAG AA contrast.
   Works whether you're wearing gloves, fighting glare on a sunny
   patio, or just want bigger numbers than the system calculator gives
   you.
2. **Talk it** — hold the mic and say "three forty-five plus six
   seventy-eight." Apple Speech transcribes locally, in English and
   Chinese. Dialog mode lets you keep going — "plus three, change to
   minus, what was that?" — without re-tapping the mic.
3. **Snap it** — point your camera at a paper receipt, electronic bill,
   price tag, or any screen with numbers on it. Apple Vision reads the
   total straight into your expression. Long-press to fix a single
   mis-recognised digit; no retyping the whole line.

On supported devices (iOS 26+ with Apple Intelligence enabled),
**AI Boost** understands more natural speech — "I bought 3 apples at
five dollars each" parses the same as "3 × 5". AI Boost runs on Apple
Foundation Models, locally on your device's Neural Engine. No cloud
roundtrip, no API call.

## Who ClarSum is for

- **Hands-busy moments** — cooking, driving, building, traveling.
- **Numbers-on-paper moments** — receipts, bills, price tags, exam papers.
- **Voice-first preference** — accessibility users, multitaskers,
  users with low vision or motor differences.
- **Privacy-first preference** — anyone who doesn't want their math
  going to a cloud they can't see.

## Privacy posture

- **Math, voice recognition, and receipt OCR all run on-device.** Apple
  Speech and Apple Vision, never the cloud.
- **ClarSum Pro** (one-time purchase): zero network requests, zero data
  collection.
- **ClarSum Free**: shows one launch-screen ad per cold start via
  Google AdMob. That single ad request is the only network call the
  app makes. Everything else stays on your device.

Full details in the [privacy policy](./PRIVACY.html).
