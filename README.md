## About Numo

Numo is built around three actions a calculator should do well:

1. **See it** — point your camera at a paper receipt, electronic bill,
   or price tag and Apple Vision reads the total straight into your
   expression. Long-press to edit a single character if a digit was
   mis-recognised.
2. **Tap or speak it** — keypad with ≥ 44pt touch targets and WCAG AA
   contrast, plus a voice mode that understands both English and
   Chinese spoken math. Dialog mode lets you keep talking for
   multi-step calculations without re-tapping the mic.
3. **Hear it** — every result is read aloud with a clean Apple voice;
   silence it from Settings if you prefer.

On supported devices (iOS 26+ with Apple Intelligence enabled),
**AI Boost** understands more natural speech — "I bought 3 apples at
five dollars each" parses the same as "3 × 5". AI Boost runs on
Apple Foundation Models, locally on your device's Neural Engine.

## Privacy posture

- **Math, voice recognition, and receipt OCR all run on-device.** Apple
  Speech and Apple Vision, never the cloud.
- **Numo Pro** (one-time purchase): zero network requests, zero data
  collection.
- **Numo Free**: shows one launch-screen ad per cold start via Google
  AdMob. That single ad request is the only network call the app
  makes. Everything else stays on your device.

Full details in the [privacy policy](./PRIVACY.html).
